Nemetra (draft)

Pig-pen and pottery village on the Luric Road, complacent while something gathers to the north.

Inhabitants: 1100 (village). Swineherds and potters, with a large fringe.

Ruler: No lord. Militia captain Durvin holds court on the village green.

Strangers: Busy and practical; nobody has time for them unless they bring coin.

Equipment: Basic. Tools, pottery, and pork plenty; iron goods scarce, +25%.

Faiths: Bandua (hearth-shrine by the great oak); Isarna (cracked waystone at the northern trailhead).

What Makes It Itself

Pigs and pots. Pork and pottery from Nemetra supply the Marches: salted pork, storage jars, roof tiles, decorated ware. The village is more prosperous than it looks, and smells worse.

By lizard-man sufferance. Nemetra exists because the lizard-men to the south allow it. There is no compact, tribute, or acknowledgement. A few elders sense it, and none say it aloud.

Something in the north. Goblin refugees have started appearing in the pig pens, gaunt and silent. The militia laughs at them. The herb-wives are studying them, and are afraid.

Anecdote: Once talked down a delirious wounded orc at the forest edge using only hand signs and offering him water. Militia wanted to kill it. She sent it limping north with bandaged leg.

Tragedy: Lost all three of her children (two daughters, one son) to fever in a single week during Year 285. She knows every herb that might have saved them—none grew close enough in time.

Tragedy: Her husband left her 40 years ago, said she 'loved plants more than people.' He remarried in Tagmira. She never speaks of him but keeps his tobacco pouch.

Tragedy: Her mentor, Old Kessa, was killed by militiamen who thought she was consorting with fey (she was gathering moonlit herbs). Yaleth found the body. Durvin's father led that patrol—she's never forgotten.
