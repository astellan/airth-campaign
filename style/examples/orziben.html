Orziben (draft)

Earth-walled Imazuran road-end town of wine, alum, and caravans that come less often.

Inhabitants: 1200 (town). Mostly Imazuran; Gallaecian vintners on the south slope. Tied with Caelrun as the largest town in the Luric.

Ruler: The Council of Houses, run in practice by the Idir family and its head, Amastan ut Idir. Watch Captain Haelis Corran answers to them.

Strangers: Welcome if they carry goods or coin. Pilgrims are tolerated and charged for everything. Anyone asking about the Council is noticed.

Equipment: Good. Imazuran goods at list price; western goods +10%.

Faiths: Malac Orthodox (Temple of the Eternal Flame); Malac Schismatic pilgrims, passing through for the Mountain of Fire; Isarna (crescents at the East Gate); New Gods and family bazinas in the Imazuran quarters.

What Makes It Itself

End of the road. The Luric Road stops here and the Sebkha road begins. Everything bound east or west changes wagons in Orziben, including Schismatic pilgrims bound for the Mountain of Fire, 120 miles east. Wine, olive oil, and alum go west; dyers across the Marches need the alum.

The Council. Orziben has no warden. The Council of Houses runs it like a family business, and the Idir family runs the Council. Favours are owed, collected, and remembered.

The caravans are thinning. The Imazuran trains come half as often as they did two years ago. Nobody in the caravan quarter will say why.

The man on the hill. A blind hermit lives in the hills north of town. Those who go up come back with answers they can't use. Parents keep their children off the north path.

Response

The watch: 18 watchmen under Captain Haelis Corran, paid by the Council.

By day: 1d4 watchmen within 1d10 minutes.

By night: 1d3 watchmen within 1d4 turns.

Reinforcements: Idir house guards, if the Council wants them sent.

Strength: 18 watchmen (founded with 40). The east wall is kept up. The west is not. The watch does what the Council says.

Bribery: 5gp makes a gate-watchman forget a cart. Nothing works against the Council's word.

Places

1.East Gate

A gate in the earthen wall onto the Sebkha road, hung with copper Isarna crescents and silver thread.

Watch: 4 watchmen by day; shut at dark.

Toll: 1sp a wagon, waived for those who leave thread at the crescents.

Haelis Corran, Captain · watch captain · independent

50s, heavy, Gallaecian, ink on his cuffs. And yet: Pays the Council's cousins on time and hates every coin of it.

“I keep the gates. Ask the Council about the rest.”

Wants: The eastern caravans back, and his watch his own again.
Doesn’t want: To find out what the Idir cousins do after dark.
Found: East Gate, checking the bolts himself.

Thinks with: money

2.West Gate

A gate in the earthen wall onto the Luric Road. Hinges rusting.

Watch: 2 watchmen by day, often 1. Opened after dark on the Council's pass.

3.The Salt Yard

A walled yard inside the East Gate where the Sebkha road ends. Half empty.

Yard: Stalls for 20 wagons; 8 in use.

Beasts: Mules, camels when the Imazuran trains come in.

Services: Stabling 2sp a night; water 1cp a beast; teamsters for hire.

Who’s passing through: Taziri's wagons; fewer Imazuran trains.

4.Pilgrim Camp

Bare ground outside the East Gate where Schismatic pilgrims sleep before the Sebkha.

Custom: Sun-disks scratched into the wall facing east. Water sold by the jar, Council price.

5.Temple of the Eternal Flame

The Orthodox temple, older than the walls. The fire is lit; nobody leads the rite.

Populace: Elders. One acolyte.

Rites: Blessings, when asked. Cerin gives them badly and takes no coin.

Hidden: The dead cleric's letters to Ilmath in Dravesa, sealed and never sent, in a chest under the altar.

Cerin · acolyte · malac orthodox

17, thin, singed sleeves. And yet: Knows the whole rite by heart. Has never led it.

“The Fire-seer is resting.”

Wants: Someone to tell him what to do.
Doesn’t want: Anyone to learn the cleric is dead.
Knows: Where the cleric's unsent letters to Ilmath are.
Found: Temple of the Eternal Flame, feeding the fire.

8.The Vine and Lantern

The town's wine is poured here.

Guests: Vintners, teamsters, and the odd traveller bound east.

Services: Private room 6sp a night; common room 3cp; meals 2cp–5sp; wine 1cp a cup.

Factions in Town

Council Of Houses. Purpose: Run Orziben, and take a share of everything that passes through. Membership: The leading Imazuran houses; the Idir family first among them. Scheme: Find out who is stopping the eastern trains, and make them pay.

Malac Orthodox. Purpose: Keep the fire lit. Membership: Elders and one acolyte. Scheme: Cerin is hiding the cleric's death until someone tells him what to do.

Ilmenor. Purpose: Unknown here. Membership: One scribe, lodging at the Vine and Lantern. Scheme: Notes who takes the north path.

Trouble

  • Imazuran caravans have halved in two years. The Council wants a name.
  • The Orthodox cleric died two months ago. Cerin and Ilmath both say nothing; Schismatic pilgrims fill the town around an empty temple.

Neighbors

Dravesa: 18 miles west via luric road.
