Caelrun

Lakeside fishing village that feeds the Marches, and does not ask who lets it.

Inhabitants: 1200 (village). Mostly fisherfolk; barley farmers along the shore; fringe camps at the bog edge.

Ruler: No lord. The great families rule in practice: the Hallans (the fleet) and the Tarnes (the grain).

Strangers: Welcome for trade. Some things are carefully not asked about, and strangers who ask are noticed.

Equipment: not recorded.

Faiths: Thulda (grey-robed funeral shrine); Ossamar (cairns on the water paths).

What Makes It Itself

Food for the Marches. Fish, eels, and barley from Caelrun feed the other Marches villages. Dried and smoked fish, fish oil, rush mats, and lake-hemp rope go out along the road. Caelrun has no smith of its own; iron, tools, and boat-nails come in by caravan.

By lizard-man sufferance. The swamp around Caelrun is lizard-man country, and the village survives because they allow it. Most residents believe their prosperity comes from hard work and good land. Sarith the Watcher, Brecht Hallan, and Ordun Tarnes know the compact's real shape and keep it unspoken.

Debt-bound. Debts pass from father to son here. Bound labourers work them off in the oil pits, and the village does not protect anyone who runs.

Ruins under the lake. The Submerged Temple-Garden lies in the northern deep. Its columns show in the shallows in a dry summer, and on moonless nights the water over it glows. The Aurka are diving it; the elders know exploring it is limited by the compact.

Aurka. Purpose: Recover pre-Silence knowledge. Membership: Ossek's node in the fishmonger's back room; Varren Muddfoot informs. Scheme: Diving the Submerged Temple-Garden; stopped at its sealed lead door. A rival team is preparing too.

Ilmenor. Purpose: Pre-Silence knowledge, under their control. Membership: An agent, quietly buying. Scheme: Watching the temple ruins from a distance.
