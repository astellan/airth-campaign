What Makes It Itself

The only stone walls in the Marches. The Bandua faithful built them and keep them, and the whole town is proud of them. The North and South Gates shut at dark.

Three faiths that do not mix. Orthodox, Schismatic, and Bandua households keep to their own streets, inns, and tables. The town is quieter than its size, because nobody is getting along.

Tar, pelts, and dye. Resin from the wood yards, pelts from the forest, and dyes from the works make Dravesa rich. Pine tar is what it is known for, and the smell of it is on everything.

Response

The watch: 20 watchmen on rotating shifts, answering to the Warden.

By day: 1d3 watchmen within 1d10 minutes.

By night: 1d3 watchmen within 1d4 turns.

Reinforcements: Vraed's 30 raiders, mustered from his hall within the hour.

Strength: 20 watchmen, plus Vraed's 30 raiders (founded with 40). Comfortable after decades of quiet. Vraed's raiders are the only ones in town who have fought.

Bribery: not recorded.

7.North and South Gates

The North and South Gates in the stone walls.

Factions in Town

Malac Orthodox. Purpose: Preserve the authentic Malac rite and outlast the schism. Membership: Elders; attendance halved in five years. Scheme: Holding on.

Malac Schismatics. Purpose: Recognition for Rakhmoth's theology as the true Malac faith. Membership: The young. Scheme: Quietly preparing for Rakhmoth at Midsummer.

Bandua Faithful. Purpose: Keep the seasonal raids going and Dravesa's fighting strength intact. Membership: About 30 initiated fighters who have raided. Scheme: Force the raid through, and move on Telvari if the Warden keeps stalling.

Trouble

  • Jorun Cadek left for the Mountain of Fire 6 weeks ago and hasn't returned. Adval blames Savar publicly. Stravar needs evidence before acting.
  • Seasonal raid stalling — two steadings won't send fighters. Vraed will move unilaterally if Stravar doesn't act soon.
  • Rakhmoth reportedly coming for Midsummer (~3 weeks). Savar is quietly preparing. Stravar doesn't know yet.
  • Orziben's Orthodox cleric is dead. Ilmath is the last. She has said nothing.
