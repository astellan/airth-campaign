Dravesa

Walled forest-edge town of tar, pelts, and three faiths that do not mix.

Inhabitants: 800 (small town). Prosperous and factionalized.

Ruler: Warden Stravar Keln, from the Warden’s Hall (Area 4).

Faiths: Malac Orthodox (flame-drop pendant), Malac Schismatic (sun-disk pendant), Bandua (spear through a skull).

Strangers: Read at a glance by their pendant, or the lack of one.

Equipment: not recorded.

Arrival

Far: pine tar · woodsmoke · stone walls, the only ones in the Marches

Near: tree line close on north and east · axe-cut stumps · steading fences

Leaving: smoke over the walls · tar on your boots for a day after

What Makes It Itself

The only stone walls in the Marches. The Bandua faithful built them and keep them, and the whole town is proud of them. The North and South Gates shut at dark.

Three faiths that do not mix. Orthodox, Schismatic, and Bandua households keep to their own streets, inns, and tables. The town is quieter than its size, because nobody is getting along.

Tar, pelts, and dye. Resin from the wood yards, pelts from the forest, and dyes from the works make Dravesa rich. Pine tar is what it is known for, and the smell of it is on everything.

Response

The watch: 20 watchmen on rotating shifts, answering to the Warden.

Arrival on the scene: 1d3 watchmen within 1d10 minutes by day, 1d4 turns at night. Vraed’s 30 raiders can be mustered within the hour.

Strength: 20 watchmen. Comfortable after decades of quiet; Vraed’s raiders are the only ones in town who have fought. Founding strength: 40.

Bribery: not recorded.

Places

1.Temple of the Eternal Flame

The Orthodox temple, where services are held at dawn, midday, and dusk. Attendance has halved in five years.

Rites: blessings 1sp; funeral rites 5sp; fire-reading with Ilmath by appointment, no fixed price.

Ilmath, High Fire-seer · high fire-seer · Malac Orthodox

Mid-70s. White robes. Clear eyes.

“I am Malac’s last credible witness.”

Wants: The Orthodox to outlast the schism.
Doesn’t want: Pity.
Knows: Savar met Jorun alone six times before the pilgrimage.
Found: Tending the temple flame, every dawn.

Oddly also: Last senior Orthodox cleric alive. Has told no one.
Dreams: A proper successor before she dies.
Thinks with: faith.
Family: Widowed; two adult children in Orziben she writes to rarely.

Anecdote: Refused to close the temple during a Bronze Host siege, and held full services with arrows in the door.

Tragedy: One of her Fire-seers defected to the Schismatics three years ago. She blamed herself.

2.House of the Mountain of Fire

A converted merchant’s house with a sun-disk above the door and an east-facing skylight. Young people are always outside.

Rites: dawn rites, free and open; private counsel with Savar, free; letters of introduction to the Mountain of Fire pilgrimage.

Savar · Schismatic preacher · Malac Schismatic

Early 30s. Lean. Friendly smile. And yet: warm to the touch, and his eyes linger half a second too long.

“Jorun’s pilgrimage was a good thing.”

Wants: Rakhmoth’s arrival. Recognition for the Schismatics.
Doesn’t want: Inquiry into Jorun Cadek.
Found: Writing to pilgrims at the Mountain of Fire.

Oddly also: Perfect recall of every conversation.
Dreams: Rakhmoth’s theology recognized before the Orthodox die out.
Thinks with: persuasion.
Family: Disowned when he joined the Schismatics. A young son, Dav, raised by his mother across town.

Anecdote: Quoted a man’s own words back to him from three years prior, verbatim, mid-argument.

Tragedy: Dav doesn’t know who his father is.

3.Vraed’s Fighting Hall

A timber hall with a practice yard and the muster point for the seasonal raid.

Interior: A Bandua shrine at the far end: a bronze spear in a basin of dried blood and ash.

Members: About 30 initiated fighters who have been on at least one raid.

Services: weapons training 1sp a week; Bandua blessing before combat, free to the faithful.

Joining: Schismatic converts are refused.

Vraed Halman · raid leader · Bandua faithful

Mid-40s. Two fingers missing, left hand. Bandua’s mark tattooed on right forearm. And yet: ink-stained fingers on the maimed hand.

“I’m the only one here taking this seriously.”

Wants: The seasonal raid. Schismatics gone before Rakhmoth.
Doesn’t want: Stravar stalling.
Offers: 4 fighters as escort.
Found: Fighting-hall yard at dawn, training alone.

Oddly also: Has never lost a fighter under his command.
Dreams: His sons leading raids after him.
Thinks with: blade.
Family: Wife Sera; two sons of raiding age; two younger daughters.

Anecdote: Lost two fingers to a goblin and killed it with the same hand.

Tragedy: Lost a close friend when a Schismatic-leaning steading refused to send warning of a raid.

4.Warden’s Hall

A stone building on the market square. Court sits three times a week; the Warden lives upstairs.

Fees: dispute resolution; letters of passage 2gp; contract notarization 5sp; public notices 1cp.

Stravar Keln, the Warden · warden · independent

Mid-50s. Compact. Scar along the jaw.

“Until someone wiser decides.”

Wants: Resolution before Rakhmoth arrives, without martyrs.
Doesn’t want: Acting without evidence.
Knows: Caravan roads and who travels them.
Offers: Coin; passage to Orziben.
Found: Warden’s Hall, sharpening his knife at dusk.

Oddly also: Keeps a private list of everyone who has travelled to the Mountain of Fire.
Dreams: Dravesa quiet enough that nobody needs a warden.
Thinks with: diplomacy.
Family: Estranged wife in Orziben; three grown children he rarely sees.

Anecdote: Talked a Bronze Host raiding party into leaving a steading unmolested, with no weapons drawn.

Tragedy: His wife left when he took this posting. He thought she would come around.

5.The Gilded Ash Inn

The best inn between Tagmira and Orziben.

Common room: Seats 60. Every faction drinks here, at tables Maret chooses.

Services: private room 8sp a night (12 rooms); common room 4cp a night; meals 3cp–8sp; stabling 2sp a night; bath 1cp.

Trouble: Recruiting is not tolerated; Maret throws out offenders without calling the watch.

Maret Duvall · innkeeper · independent

40s. Strong hands. Ale-stained apron.

“Leave your god at the door.”

Wants: Her inn kept theology-free.
Doesn’t want: Factions recruiting in her common room.
Knows: Everything said in her common room.
Offers: Gossip, for a story.
Found: Behind the bar; account books at close.

Oddly also: Knows which faction every regular belongs to and seats them accordingly.
Dreams: Her daughter takes over the inn; her son goes somewhere safer.
Thinks with: money.
Family: Husband Bren runs the kitchen; 2 teenagers work the inn. Raised Orthodox; never speaks of it.

Anecdote: Threw Vraed’s men and two Schismatics out at once last Waterday, without calling the watch.

Tragedy: Her brother joined the Schismatics last year. They haven’t spoken since.

6.Cadek Merchant House

A grain and cargo brokerage, shuttered for six weeks.

Wares: grain, preserved goods, cargo brokering. Closed.

Adval Cadek · merchant · independent

50s. Prosperous build gone soft. And yet: still dressed immaculately.

“I should have seen it.”

Wants: Proof that Savar sent his son to his death.
Doesn’t want: To hear that Jorun went willingly.
Offers: Coin, for proof.

Oddly also: Warehouse shut six weeks. Losing money daily; doesn’t care.
Dreams: Jorun comes home.
Thinks with: guilt.
Family: Wife Pemma, holding the household together; Jorun (son, missing, 21); two younger daughters.

Anecdote: Built the Cadek house from a single mule cart twenty years ago.

Tragedy: Jorun left for the Mountain of Fire six weeks ago and has not returned.

7.North and South Gates

Watch: 2 watchmen each by day; shut at dark, and opened after only on the Warden’s pass.

Factions in Town

Malac Orthodox. Purpose: preserve the authentic Malac rite and outlast the schism. Membership: elders, attendance halved in five years. Current scheme: holding on. Leader: Ilmath (Area 1).

Malac Schismatics. Purpose: recognition for Rakhmoth’s theology as the true Malac faith. Membership: the young. Current scheme: quietly preparing for Rakhmoth at Midsummer. Leader: Savar (Area 2).

Bandua Faithful. Purpose: keep the seasonal raids going and Dravesa’s fighting strength intact. Membership: about 30 initiated fighters who have raided. Current scheme: force the raid through, and move on Telvari if the Warden keeps stalling. Leader: Vraed (Area 3).

Trouble

  • Jorun Cadek left for the Mountain of Fire 6 weeks ago and hasn’t returned. Adval blames Savar publicly; Stravar needs evidence before acting.
  • The seasonal raid is stalling because two steadings won’t send fighters. Vraed will move alone if Stravar doesn’t act soon.
  • Rakhmoth is reportedly coming for Midsummer, about 3 weeks away. Savar is preparing quietly; Stravar doesn’t know yet.
  • Orziben’s Orthodox cleric is dead, leaving Ilmath the last. She has said nothing.

Neighbors

Steadings

Telvari: a forest-edge steading and Schismatic centre of fire worship, where humans and humanoids meet. Kind and why-here not recorded.
