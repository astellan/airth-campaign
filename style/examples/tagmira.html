Tagmira (draft)

Hardscrabble upland village under the Bramblewood, holding off orcs it no longer understands.

Inhabitants: 320 (village). Herders and farmers; clannish.

Ruler: No lord. The elders, with militia captain Hadran Throsk holding informal court in the hearth-hall.

Strangers: The party is known: owed for the northwest, and blamed for the dead. Hostility sits just under the surface.

Equipment: not recorded.

Faiths: Cossus (the stone circle; trial by combat); Thulda (the gravefield and the Mound of Threads); Isarna (the waystone); Ossamar (the ford, being restored).

What Makes It Itself

The dead walk. Everyone knows the dead walk in the Bramblewood, and some of Tagmira's own recent dead have gone there. Doors are barred at dusk.

Owed and blamed. The party pushed the Bronze Host back and retook the northwestern hunting grounds, and the village will eat this winter because of it. Their alliance with the dead is known. Some are grateful; others blame them for the dead who walked into the wood.

Black legs. Whole legs of mutton and goat are salted and cold-smoked for months over ridge juniper at Carnaxe. Black legs are the local delicacy and the reason caravans stop. Hunters' hams of venison and boar cost more. Blood sausage is eaten fresh at the autumn slaughter and never sold; a visitor offered some is being honoured.

Sacred ground everywhere. A Cossus stone ring for trial by combat, a Thulda gravefield, a mound of wool offerings, an Isarna waystone, and a ford whose cold shrine is being restored. The village keeps them all.

Factions in Town

Village Militia. Purpose: Keep the orcs cowed and the village fed. Membership: 7 standing, 6 veterans. Scheme: Hold the retaken northwest hunting grounds.

Tagmira Hunters. Purpose: Feed the village and keep the northwest. Membership: About a dozen hunters, Cael Gald among them. Scheme: Hunt the far grounds without the militia's leave, and sell the game to Carnaxe.

Trouble

  • The Bronze Host was pushed back from the northwest, but all its leaders survived.
  • The party's alliance with the dead is known, and resented by some; a few blame the party for the dead who went to the Bramblewood.
  • Maedra has vanished — whispers but no answers. The undead want more Tagmiran recruits.
  • Two Bronze Host orcs were brought to Tagmira as captives to witness the trial by combat — meant as intimidation. It may have given the orcs useful intelligence about Tagmira's current strength instead.
  • The water-rights grudge persists after the duel. Tenn Darnel lost and has not forgiven Ulan Caernach.
  • Ulan Caernach and Rondasek Elaran are back to village life but changed — they've seen skeletons fight and orcs bleed. They are quietly restless.
  • Salt comes only by caravan. Taziri, Aelis Varrow, and the Aredos haggle over it every curing season.
  • The militia resents the Aredos buying game from the hunters, off grounds the militia bled to retake.
